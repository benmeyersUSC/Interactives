Likelihood bench

One expression, f(x | θ), read two ways. Fix θ and it describes data. Fix the data and it ranks θ. Everything below is that sentence made draggable.

f(x | θ) with θ fixed

A distribution over outcomes. Sums or integrates to 1 across x.

L(θ) = f(x | θ) with x fixed

A ranking of candidate θ. Normalises to nothing in particular, and never needs to.

the θ that generated the data your candidate θ̂, on the slider the maximiser the data picks out
Datasets are drawn from the carmine parameters with a seeded generator, then held fixed — that is what makes L a function of θ alone. Drag directly on the lower three plots to move θ̂.
