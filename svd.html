Turn off vary tilt and the orbit becomes pure rotation: U and V spin, Σ sits perfectly still. Turn it back on and only σ₁ responds. The hairline marks σ = 1. Only σ₁ moves, sweeping out to 1/cos α; σ₂ sits on the mark for every beam angle, because a sphere's shadow always has the sphere's own width across the tilt. The whole family of shadows is one stretch and a rotation.
